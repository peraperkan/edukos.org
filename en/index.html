Welcome
to EDUKOS.

A good idea can begin with a question, a classroom conversation, or a wish to make the spaces we share more welcoming. At EDUKOS, a citizens’ association based in Kragujevac, we help those ideas take shape through learning, creativity, and working together.

From programming and robotics workshops through ProgramiGranje to Green Oasis, a popular science library, and illuminated portraits of scientists in a school classroom, we look for ways to bring knowledge into everyday life. Sometimes that means giving old equipment a new purpose; sometimes it means opening a door to a world a child is only beginning to discover.

That curiosity continues through the Science for Young People portal and the development of HEM, a strategy and educational board game. We want science to feel understandable and exciting, history to inspire exploration, and learning to bring the pleasure of discovery. Explore our projects, find something that sparks your interest, and meet the people and ideas behind them. Welcome to EDUKOS.
