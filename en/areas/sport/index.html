Sport

Movement that strengthens people and communities.

Sport and recreation encourage an active life and create opportunities to socialize and work together. Promoting them is among EDUKOS’s aims, alongside education and community development. Through sport, we learn perseverance, cooperation, respect for rules and fair treatment of others. We value physical activity that is encouraging and accessible to people of different ages and abilities.
