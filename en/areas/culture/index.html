Culture

Discovering our heritage and learning about others.

Culture preserves a community’s stories, values and creativity, while connecting us with other people and their experiences. EDUKOS’s aims include promoting culture and working with organizations and individuals who share this interest. Exploring cultural heritage develops a sense of belonging, while encountering different cultures encourages openness and mutual respect. Culture thrives when we explore, create and share it.
