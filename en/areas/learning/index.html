Learning

Curiosity is where new knowledge begins.

Learning helps us understand the world, develop skills and make independent decisions. At EDUKOS, it plays an important role in our aim to improve learning opportunities for children, young people and adults. Workshops, professional gatherings and the exchange of experience envisioned in our Statute create space for learning through conversation and practical work. We believe that opportunities to learn something new should be available throughout life.
