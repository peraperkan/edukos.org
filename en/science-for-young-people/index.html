Welcome to NZM — Science for Young People, a place for questions, discoveries, and the pleasure of understanding how the world works. Explore the fascinating side of science through engaging articles, science games, and interactive activities, explained in a simple, clear, and relaxed way. Follow the ideas that changed history, experiment with interactive examples, and find a topic that makes you want to learn more. NZM is a volunteer project of the EDUKOS association, developed and maintained by Miloš Milosavljević out of a love of science and technology and a desire to share that enthusiasm with others. The project also brings together EDUKOS and the First Kragujevac Gymnasium. Whether you are still at school or simply enjoy asking “why?”, you are welcome here.
